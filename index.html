Important Grant Policy & Fee Guidelines
  • No Approval, No Fee: If your application is not approved, no payment will be required under any circumstances.
  • Fee Payment Upon Approval: Application & processing fees ($500) are required only after approval and must be paid to your assigned agent via your official PT Grant email instructions.
  • Doorstep Delivery Support: Any grant option requiring physical delivery will have delivery costs fully covered using allocated assistance funds.
  • 100% Cash Refund Eligibility: Cash assistance and grant fees are fully refundable under verified program guidelines.
